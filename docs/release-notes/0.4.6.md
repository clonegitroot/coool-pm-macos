## Coool PM 0.4.6

- **Quieter backup reminders.** Coool PM only reminds you on close if your recovery copy is out of date and you have not backed up within the chosen interval. The default is two days. Change it under **Settings → Library → Backup reminder on close**, or choose **Never**. Dismissing a reminder postpones it for the same interval.
- **Fresh setup after a full reset.** **Reset app and library** now creates a recovery backup and returns to the recovery-key setup screen. Keep your previous key to open backups made before the reset.
